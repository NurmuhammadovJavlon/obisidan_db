# Wealth

>[[Napoleon Hill's Timeless Thoughts for Today - Napoleon Hill Judith Williamson]]

Napoleon Hill’s method by which desire for riches can be transmuted into its financial
equivalent, consists of six definite, practical steps, viz:

**First**: fix in your mind the exact amount of money you desire. It is not sufficient merely
to say “I want plenty of money.” Be definite as to the amount.

$ 1 000 000

**Second**: determine exactly what you intend to give in return for the money you desire.

1 year of hard work, determination

**Third**: establish a definite date when you intend to possess the money you desire.

11 07 2027

**Fourth**: create a definite plan for carrying out your desire, and begin at once, whether
you are ready or not, to put this plan into action.

- $10 000 x 100 people
- $5000 x 200 people
- $1000 x 1000 people
- $100 x 10 000 people
- $10 x 100 000 people
- $1 x 1 000 000 people

**Fifth**: write out a clear, concise statement of the amount of money you intend to acquire,
name the time limit for its acquisition, state what you intend to give in return for the
money, and describe clearly the plan through which you intend to accumulate it.

**Sixth**: read your written statement aloud, twice daily, once just before retiring at night,
and once after arising in the morning. As you read—see and feel and believe yourself
already in possession of the money.

# What’s Your Alibi?

A good spot check on the alibis you live by can be achieved by creating a list of your
goals on the left-hand side of a sheet of paper. List 10 to 20 goals in column fashion.
Now, on the right hand side, consider what keeps you from achieving these goals. This
is where your alibis will rear their ugly heads. Once you see them for what they are—
big drawbacks to your success—you need to eradicate them one by one. Only when you
pull them up by their roots will you be rid of them forever.
