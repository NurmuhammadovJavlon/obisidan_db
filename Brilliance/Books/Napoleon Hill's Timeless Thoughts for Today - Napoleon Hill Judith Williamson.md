## Key takeaways

- Stay away from negativity, always strive to be opportunistic
- Stay away from quarreling with people as it will have a negative impact on your mental health, puts unnecesary stress on you and you will loose so much energy trying to prove yourself. YOU DON’T HAVE TO PROVE YOURSELF.
- Build long lasting relationships by showing some effort such as visiting them, calling/texting the people you care. Having a good network is useful
- **Your only and best competitor is YOU, always. Become better than yesterday because you are limitless**
- Lead with Actions not words
- Don’t let others to distract you from your mission. Remember, you don’t have a time for little petty things, Your mission is the most important! Don’t disturb others on their mission too.
- Accentuate the Positive, Eliminate the Negative
- Earn your Confidence by doing the things you said you would do and need to Do
- One worry brings many. If you want to play “what if?” game, Play it to WIN! Don’t say what if it doesn’t work, instead say what if it works, what if I succeed…
- A true leader leads by example
- Progress > Perfection. You can do anything, Anything. A big task can be broken down into small chunks and sequential steps. Perfect idea doesn’t exist, there are only better ones.
- Love and respect starts with yourself. Take time to care about the inner you and outer you will shine bright
- If you can’t say something nice about someone, don’t say anything at all. When they gossip about someone and talk negatively, remain quiet or walk away
- Fear is the beginning of all bad. Faith is fear in reverse. Depression is an expression of fear. Fear from your Lord.
- To avoid negativity, make your hands busy with labor. You may go for a car wash, do some exercise.
- You are in a war against your limited self. He is negative, emotional, stupid and lazy. To conquer him, you must first acknowledge his existence, separate him from your true self which is in tune with the source. In your journals, label him as “Weak Goblin”(Devil) and write down your journey with him about how “The Source” is trying to help you win in this war.

Always remember this: **“And when My servants ask you about Me, indeed I am near. I respond to the call of the caller when he calls upon Me.”**— Qur'an 2:186

- Wherever I go, I meet Allah, the Almighty. Whenever the Goblin takes over your mind, think about that Allah created you with unlimited amount of love. I can’t assume anything about my Lord, but when you look how people feel, think when they are creating something they usually get filled with love, they expect the best of the best from their creation. Creation is an expression of love.
- Those who refuse to learn from history are doomed to repeat it.
- Never criticize. It does not work. Make sure you are a builder, not a destroyer of people and things.
- Nothing comes of nothing. It is in giving that we receive
- Through force of habit, develop a character which will act appropriate always, even in insignificant moments. you will do the right thing—without thinking—in important situations. Actions are like lightning. They impact our lives. Lightning strikes where you take action.
- Be crystal clear about what you desire. List makers know this to be true. When you construct either a to-do list or an I-dream list, you keep the concept of your desire front and center in your mind’s eye.
- This is a fine world for the person who knows precisely what he or she expects from life and is busy getting it
- You must be happy before you can spread the joy.
- **People of character do what they say they will do, when they say they will do it, and according to agreed-upon conditions.**
- KEEP YOUR INTEGRITY
- It has been said that we are the sum total of our thoughts to date.
- Don’t be Devil’s advocate!!! Don’t seek out the negative in the positive but rather condition your mind to focus only on the positive aspects of everything.
- The surest way to control your faults is to attack them the moment they appear. Write down your faults when you see them, analyze and find ways to replace them with good virtues. -> [[The Character - Qualities from THE SOURCE]]
- Dr. V . Frankl tells us in [[Man's Search for Meaning - Viktor E Frankl]], the last thing we can control when all else is taken away, is our attitude. 
- Be a completer, a doer, an action oriented person. Think of it this way, what happens when a gentleman caller fails to bring candy and flowers to his beloved on Valentine’s Day, but states that he had the greatest intention of doing so?
- Persistence is the key.
- When people begin to complain, gossip, and downgrade life in general, take this as your cue to leave the premises. Nothing good is going to show up as long as you are in bad company. Give yourself marching orders and step out of negativity into the positive future that you foresee for yourself. Even if you are traveling alone, it is well worth the trip.

### What’s Your Alibi?
A good spot check on the alibis you live by can be achieved by creating a list of your
goals on the left-hand side of a sheet of paper. List 10 to 20 goals in column fashion.
Now, on the right hand side, consider what keeps you from achieving these goals. This
is where your alibis will rear their ugly heads. Once you see them for what they are—
big drawbacks to your success—you need to eradicate them one by one. Only when you
pull them up by their roots will you be rid of them forever.
