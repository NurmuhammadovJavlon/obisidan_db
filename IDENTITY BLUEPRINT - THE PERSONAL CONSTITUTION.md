# IDENTITY V1.0
## I. - "anti-identity"

This behavioral strategy no longer serves the person I'm building

- Why begin with what I don't want to become?

Because before you can build a life, you need to know what you will not sell yourself for.

You will face opportunities, temptations, fear, comfort, rejection, money, attraction, pressure, uncertainty, and failure in your lifetime

In those moments, you may not remember every goal you have written. But you can remember:

**“This is not who I am”**

Your identity needs boundaries.

- So this isn't a list of things I hate about myself?

No.

You are not declaring war on yourself.

You are declaring war on the patterns that make you smaller, weak.

There is a difference.

You can forgive the person you were while refusing to remain him.

### I.I REFUSE TO BE A SLAVE TO MY IMPULSES

- Sometimes I know what I should do, but I still choose what feels good now.

Then understand this clearly:

Every time you repeatedly obey an impulse against your own judgment, you teach yourself that your desires are stronger than your decisions.

And every time you act according to your principles despite an impulse, you teach yourself:

**“I command myself.”**

You will still experience laziness.

You will still experience lust, anger, fear, boredom, distraction, insecurity and the desire for comfort.

The goal is not to stop being human.

The goal is to stop being **ruled by whatever you happen to feel.**
